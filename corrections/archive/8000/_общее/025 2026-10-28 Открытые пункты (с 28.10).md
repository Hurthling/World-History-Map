  - EN: "Named after Golondrina points (Spanish for 'swallow'): their concave base with flaring 'ears' recalls a swallow's forked tail."
  - В SOURCES правила `m8-golondrina` добавить https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golondrina_point
